The TurnKey Difference

Good sod is the easy part. Grading, tight seams, and a proper first soak are what make a lawn root fast and stay green through a Gulf-Coast summer.

01 — Prep

We get the ground right first

Before a single roll goes down, the site is cleared, loosened, graded, and raked smooth. On our heavy clay soils, drainage and grade matter as much as the grass itself.

  • Debris & old turf cleared
  • Soil loosened and leveled
  • Drainage checked for flat NOLA lots
02 — Lay

Fresh sod, tight seams, rolled firm

Pallets are cut close to delivery day and staged in shade. Pieces are laid along a straight edge with staggered, tightly butted seams, then the whole lawn is rolled into firm contact.

  • Cut fresh, never left to dry
  • Staggered, seamless joints
  • Rolled for root-to-soil contact
03 — Root

A lawn that takes hold

We water in deeply the same day and send you off with a simple first-month schedule. New sod roots in about two to three weeks and is ready for its first mow soon after.

  • Deep first-day soak
  • First-month care plan
  • Best-price guarantee
